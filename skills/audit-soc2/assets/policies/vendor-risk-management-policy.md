## 1. Purpose

Defines how third-party vendors are assessed for security risk before onboarding and
periodically thereafter.

## 2. Scope

Applies to any vendor that processes, stores, or has access to [PLACEHOLDER: customer data /
production systems].

## 3. Policy statements

- **Pre-onboarding assessment.** Before engaging a new vendor with access to [PLACEHOLDER: data
  category], [PLACEHOLDER: role] reviews the vendor's own security posture — e.g. their SOC 2
  report if available, or a security questionnaire if not.
- **Subservice organization classification.** Vendors relevant to the in-scope system are
  classified as carve-out or inclusive per `references/scoping.md`; the decision and rationale
  are recorded in the vendor register.
- **Periodic re-assessment.** Vendors are re-reviewed [PLACEHOLDER: annually or at contract
  renewal].
- **Offboarding.** Access granted to a vendor is revoked when the relationship ends, following
  the same process as the Access Control Policy.

## 4. Vendor register

Maintained at [PLACEHOLDER: location], recording per vendor: name, data/access scope,
assessment date, subservice classification, next review date.
