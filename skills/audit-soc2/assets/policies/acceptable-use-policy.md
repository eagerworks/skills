contractors.

## 2. Scope

Applies to all personnel with access to company systems, including personal devices used for
work (if permitted).

## 3. Policy statements

- **Device security.** Devices accessing company systems must have [PLACEHOLDER: disk
  encryption, screen lock, up-to-date OS patches] enforced.
- **Account use.** Credentials are individual and not shared; MFA is used where required by the
  Access Control Policy.
- **Data handling.** Company and customer data is handled per the Data Classification &
  Retention Policy — not copied to unapproved personal storage or third-party tools.
- **Software.** Only [PLACEHOLDER: approved / reasonably vetted] software is installed on
