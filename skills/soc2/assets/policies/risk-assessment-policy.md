> **Auditor note:** CC3 requires a documented process, not just a one-time risk list — auditors
> look for evidence the assessment is actually re-run, not a document written once for the audit
> and never revisited.

## 1. Purpose

Defines how [Your Company, Inc.] identifies, evaluates, and addresses risks to
[PLACEHOLDER: system boundary], including risk from change and fraud risk.

## 2. Scope

Applies to [PLACEHOLDER: system boundary] and the business processes supporting it.

## 3. Process

1. **Identify** risks — including technical (e.g. infrastructure, third-party dependency),
   operational (e.g. key-person dependency), and fraud-related risks.
2. **Assess** likelihood and impact for each identified risk.
3. **Determine treatment** — mitigate, accept, transfer, or avoid — with rationale.
4. **Track** open risks and their treatment status until resolved or formally accepted.
5. **Re-run** the full assessment at least [PLACEHOLDER: annually], and whenever a significant
   change occurs (new infrastructure, new data type handled, material incident).

## 4. Risk register

Maintained at [PLACEHOLDER: location], recording per risk: description, category, likelihood,
impact, treatment, owner, status.
